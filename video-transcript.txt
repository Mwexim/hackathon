[0:00–0:08] SLIDE 1 — COVER

A major life event rarely happens in one transaction.
This is KBC Life Moments: from financial signals to meaningful moments of help.


[0:08–0:25] SLIDE 2 — A LIFE EVENT IS NEVER ONE TRANSACTION

Moving house leaves small signals behind.
The old rent stops. A new one starts. A first bill arrives from a new energy provider.
A question to Kate: "How do I change my address?"
Alone, none of these proves anything. Together, they tell a story.


[0:25–0:45] SLIDE 3 — ONE SHARED LAYER

Life Moments is a shared layer that connects these signals and tries to understand
what might be changing in a customer's life.
It doesn't decide what to sell. Once a customer confirms a moment, every KBC service
decides for itself how it can help: insurance, savings, home and mortgage,
financial planning. Customers no longer have to figure out which product they need.


[0:45–1:00] SLIDE 4 — KBC NEVER ASSUMES

This only works if customers stay in control. So there's one principle: KBC never assumes.
We observe. We suggest a simple question. We explain why we asked.
The customer confirms, dismisses, or simply ignores it.
Only then do we help: first by protecting what they already have.
Health payments are never used.


[1:00–1:15] SLIDE 5 — BUILT FOR 2.3 MILLION CUSTOMERS

Every customer is scored on their own with a light, explainable model,
so it simply runs in parallel.
And it gets smarter as it grows: every yes or no is feedback on the signals behind it.
The more customers answer, the sharper each signal becomes,
and the fewer, better questions we ask.


[1:15–1:20] SLIDE 6 — LET'S MEET SARAH

Let's meet Sarah. She just had a baby.


[1:20–2:15] SCREEN RECORDING — SARAH

(Dashboard -> "Something may have changed" card -> notification)
KBC noticed something, but it doesn't assume. So it asks: has your family grown?

(Open "Why am I seeing this?")
Sarah can see exactly why: child benefit started, a new childcare payment,
and her family insurance still covers only two people.
Her hospital bill is never used.

(Tap "Yes, that's right")
She confirms. It's now a confirmed life moment: a new baby.

(Suggestions -> "Add your child to your family insurance" -> 2 -> 3 -> Confirm change)
The first thing KBC does is protect what she already has.
Her child is added to her family insurance: one tap, done.

(Back to suggestions)
Only then an offer, and just one, because Sarah chose light suggestions.


[2:15–2:27] SCREEN RECORDING — THOMAS

(Thomas confirms "Are you travelling more often?")
Thomas travels a lot. He confirms, and KBC tells him he's already covered
by his credit card. No sale.
Sometimes the best help is: you don't need anything.


[2:27–2:45] SCREEN RECORDING — KBC EMPLOYEE VIEW

(Dashboard -> move the threshold slider -> open "Explain the math")
On KBC's side, the same engine scans thousands of customers in a fraction of a second.
KBC decides how sure the system must be before it asks,
and can see the reasoning behind every single question.


[2:45–3:00] BACK TO SLIDE 1 — COVER

Banking shouldn't start with "Which product are you looking for?"
It should start with a much simpler question:
what has changed in your life, and how can we help?
KBC Life Moments.
